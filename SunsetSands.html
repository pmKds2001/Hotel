Hikkaduwa Beach

Hikkaduwa Beach boasts golden sands, crystal-clear waters, and gentle waves. Perfect for relaxing, swimming, snorkeling, or enjoying stunning sunsets, it offers an unforgettable coastal experience full of natural beauty and tropical charm.

Learn More

Galle Dutch Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage fortress located only minutes from our hotel. Wander ancient cobblestone streets, colonial ramparts, ocean-view lighthouses, artisan cafes, and experience Sri Lanka's living history.

Learn More

Sea Turtle Sanctuary

Experience gentle giant green sea turtles swimming right along the shoreline in Hikkaduwa. Snorkel through colorful coral reefs and discover marine conservation efforts up close in tranquil turquoise lagoons.
